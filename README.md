# River EV Sales & Market Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI portfolio project exploring sales, estimated
costs and profit, discounts, customer ratings, delivery status, regional
performance, sales channels, and River Indie colour preferences.

> **Dataset disclaimer:** This project uses a **synthetic dataset
> created for portfolio and dashboard-development purposes**.
> Transaction records, order values, costs, estimated profits,
> discounts, ratings, delivery details, and regional results are
> illustrative---not official River Mobility data and not a
> representation of the company's actual business performance.

## Project Objectives

-   Analyse order value and estimated profit trends over time.
-   Compare performance across states, cities, and sales channels.
-   Explore order distribution by customer type and River Indie colour.
-   Review discounts, accessory value, customer ratings, and delivery
    status.
-   Present results in a consolidated Power BI dashboard.

## Dashboard Highlights

The supplied dashboard image shows:

-   **KPI cards:** transaction count, sales, estimated cost, estimated
    profit, discounts, customer rating, and selected vehicle metrics.
-   **Delivery status:** a doughnut chart for delivered, cancelled,
    booking-confirmed, and in-transit records.
-   **Sales trends:** a monthly sales chart with month and quarter
    context.
-   **Regional performance:** sales by state and quantity by state.
-   **Transaction detail table:** sales channel, city, quantity,
    customer type, colour, total sales, and accessory value.
-   **Product context:** River Indie scooter imagery and selected
    product information.
-   **Colour-focused views:** dashboard variations showing different
    scooter colours and related sample order distributions.

## Dataset and Data Integrity

The dataset is synthetic. Totals and patterns shown in the dashboard are
results of constructed sample data and must not be cited as real River
sales figures.

Fields such as estimated product cost and estimated gross profit use
modelling assumptions. They demonstrate Power BI calculations but are
not verified company financial data. Check River's official materials
before presenting product specifications or current prices as factual or
current.

## Suggested Business Questions

1.  How does monthly order value vary across the selected period?
2.  Which states contribute the most order value in this sample?
3.  Which sales channels appear most frequently?
4.  Which customer types and scooter colours occur most often?
5.  How are records distributed across delivery statuses?
6.  How do discounts and accessory value relate to estimated gross
    profit?
7.  How do customer ratings vary across the sample?
